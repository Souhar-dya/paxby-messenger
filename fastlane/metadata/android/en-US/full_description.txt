Paxby is a privacy-first, offline-capable peer-to-peer messenger that discovers nearby devices over Wi-Fi Direct and exchanges end-to-end encrypted messages with no internet connection, user accounts, or central servers required.

Features:
- Peer-to-peer messaging over Wi-Fi Direct
- End-to-end encryption with per-identity keys
- Store-and-forward relay to deliver messages across multiple hops
- QR-based friend adding
- Works fully offline between nearby devices
- No telemetry, trackers, or third-party dependencies
- No Google Play Services required

Paxby is designed for private, resilient communication in offline or network-constrained environments without relying on cloud-based messengers or phone numbers.

Its unique approach combines Wi-Fi Direct discovery, multi-hop store-and-forward relay, and QR-based trust establishment, all working without any server infrastructure whatsoever.
